💻 Desktop Chrome, Edge & Brave Windows / Mac / Linux

Install as a desktop window with taskbar pinning and cinema fullscreen:

1
Look at the right side of the URL Address Bar at the top of your browser for the Install icon ( ⊕ or 💻⬇️ ).
2
Or click your browser's Menu ( ⋮ or ⋯ ) in the top right corner, then select "Save and share" ➔ "Install Brian's Theater" (or "Install Brian's Theater...").
3
Click "Install". Brian's Theater opens in its own borderless window!

2
Select "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
3
Confirm by tapping "Install". The app icon will be added to your home screen.
